## Audio

The 225 clips in `audio/` are machine-generated speech, produced once at build time
by `fetch-audio.ps1` and committed as static files. No human recordings are used and
the app never contacts a speech service at runtime.

## Code

Vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No framework, no build step, no third-party
libraries of any kind.
